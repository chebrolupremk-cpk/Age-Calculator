About Age Calculator

Our Age Calculator helps you calculate your age accurately based on your date of birth. It shows your age in years, months and days and also provides additional information such as your day of birth and upcoming birthday.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  3. View your exact age and other results.

What Does the Calculator Show?

  • Your age in years, months and days.
  • The day of the week you were born.
  • The number of days until your next birthday.
  • The approximate total number of days you have lived.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date.

Is this Age Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be free and easy to use.

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.
