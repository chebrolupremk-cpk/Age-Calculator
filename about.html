About Age Calculator

Welcome to our Age Calculator.

Our goal is to provide a simple and easy-to-use tool for calculating age based on a person's date of birth.

The calculator provides your age in years, months and days, your day of birth, your next birthday, and your approximate total number of days lived.

We designed this website to be simple, fast and accessible on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Thank you for using our Age Calculator.
